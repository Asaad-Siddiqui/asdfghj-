# TRAVELLO — Rewards, Impact and Leaderboard Context

## Purpose

Rewards make responsible actions visible and motivating.

Rewards should reinforce behavior, not become the sole purpose of the platform.

## MVP rewards

Implement:
- impact points
- badges
- challenge completion
- progress
- leaderboard

Avoid:
- crypto
- blockchain
- tokens
- complicated financial reward systems

## Points

Each challenge has a point value.

On successful completion:
- create completion record
- award points once
- update user impact total
- evaluate badge rules

Prevent duplicate awards.

## Badge examples

- Responsible Traveler
- Waste Warrior
- Water Saver
- Trail Guardian
- Nature Respecter
- Support Local

## Impact screen

Show:
- total impact points
- challenges completed
- badges
- destinations
- recent achievements

## Leaderboard

Views:
- monthly
- overall
- destination

Leaderboard should be encouraging rather than shaming.

## Achievement share

Completion should produce a shareable achievement card containing:
- user
- challenge
- destination
- points
- badge
- optional travel image

## Future rewards

Possible:
- partner discounts
- sustainable business rewards
- certificates
- sponsored challenges

These are future scope.
