# TRAVELLO — Sustainable Business Context

## Purpose

Connect travelers with hospitality providers that expose useful sustainability and accessibility information.

## Business types

- hotels
- resorts
- restaurants
- local guides
- local experiences

## Business card

Show:
- image
- name
- type
- destination
- sustainability score/index
- accessibility summary
- key practices

## Business profile

Example:
Green Valley Resort

Sustainability:
82 / 100

Practices:
- waste management
- water conservation
- energy efficiency
- plastic reduction
- refill stations

Accessibility:
- accessible entrance
- accessible washroom
- lift
- accessible rooms where applicable

## Trust

Business-provided information must not automatically be described as independently verified.

Use labels such as:
- Business provided
- Platform index
- Verified by partner, only if actually implemented

## MVP

Use curated demo businesses.

Do not build:
- complete hotel PMS
- full booking engine
- payment gateway
- inventory management

The business module supports the PS5 ecosystem but is not the core demo.
