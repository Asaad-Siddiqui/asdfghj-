# TRAVELLO — Accessibility Context

## Purpose

Accessibility is a first-class travel decision, not a decorative badge.

The platform should help travelers understand whether a destination or business meets their actual needs.

## Information categories

Destination:
- wheelchair suitability
- accessible routes
- accessible toilets
- parking
- terrain/trail difficulty
- transport limitations

Business:
- accessible entrance
- lift
- accessible washroom
- accessible rooms
- parking
- mobility information

Where appropriate, future categories can include:
- visual accessibility
- hearing accessibility
- family suitability
- sensory considerations

## Important UX rule

Never simply say:
“Accessible”

without explaining what that means.

Prefer:
“Accessible entrance: Yes”
“Accessible toilet: No”
“Wheelchair trail: Limited”

## Filters

Travelers may filter destinations/businesses by accessibility needs.

The MVP can support a small set of practical filters.

## Trust

Accessibility information may come from:
- business
- destination manager
- traveler report
- partner verification

Display source labels when useful.

## Report accessibility issue

Users should be able to report:
- blocked accessible route
- broken ramp
- inaccessible facility
- inaccurate listing

This connects accessibility with the Report & Protect system.
