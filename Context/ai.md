# TRAVELLO — AI and Intelligence Context

## Philosophy

Use AI where it improves decisions or reduces friction.

Do not add AI simply to say the product uses AI.

## MVP recommendation engine

A rule-based engine is sufficient.

Inputs:
- destination
- visitor pressure
- environmental factors
- traveler preferences
- accessibility requirements
- challenge history

Example:
High visitor pressure
→ recommend low-impact/off-peak challenge

Wildlife destination
→ recommend wildlife-respect challenge

Water stress
→ recommend water-saving challenge

## Explainability

Every recommendation should have a simple reason.

Example:
“Recommended because this destination currently has high visitor pressure.”

## AI-assisted future features

Potential:
- conversational destination assistant
- personalized responsible itinerary suggestions
- report categorization
- issue summarization
- evidence assistance
- natural-language travel recommendations

## Report categorization

An AI model could classify a report into:
- waste
- accessibility
- overcrowding
- infrastructure
- environmental damage

For MVP, manual category selection or simple rules are sufficient.

## Destination intelligence

AI can summarize:
- frequently reported issues
- emerging hotspots
- recent changes

Do not present AI-generated summaries as authoritative facts without source data.

## Privacy

Do not send sensitive personal data to third-party AI services unnecessarily.

## Product rule

The core product must remain functional without AI.
