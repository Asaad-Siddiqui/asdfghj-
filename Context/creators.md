# TRAVELLO — Creator and Ambassador Context

## Purpose

Travel creators are behavior multipliers.

The concept is:
Traditional travel influence: “Go here.”
TRAVELLO influence: “Go here, and experience it responsibly.”

## Creator profile

Show:
- avatar
- name/handle
- creator status
- followers
- destinations
- responsible challenges
- campaigns
- posts

## Responsible Travel Ambassador

A creator may be labelled:
Responsible Travel Creator
or
Responsible Travel Ambassador

Use the label consistently.

## Campaign

Campaign fields:
- title
- creator
- destination
- description
- cover image
- associated challenges
- participant count
- start/end dates if used
- status

Example:
Matheran Responsible Weekend

## Campaign page

Show:
- creator
- goal
- destination
- challenges
- participant count
- Join Campaign CTA
- progress

## Creator journey

Create campaign
→ publish
→ followers discover
→ followers join
→ participants complete challenges
→ campaign participation increases

## MVP scope

A simple creator campaign is enough.

Do not build:
- full influencer marketplace
- brand contracts
- payment settlement
- sponsorship management

## Social proof

Show participation counts and achievements, but label prototype/demo metrics if simulated.
